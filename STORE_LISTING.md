Draft copy to paste into Play Console / App Store Connect. Edit freely.

**Title (30 chars max):** Woof Wars: Idle Dog Army
**Short description (80 max):** Merge weapons, command a pack of dogs, and hold off zombies and robots.

**Full description**
A pack of brave dogs is all that stands between your base and a horde of zombies and robots.

Buy weapons, merge matching pairs into stronger ones, and watch your pack fire away on its own. Even when you close the game, your dogs keep earning.

- Merge your way up a 13-step weapon ladder, from a thrown rock to a grenade launcher
- Collect dog guardians: Rudy, Cooper, Bruno, Luna and more, each with its own look and perk
- Battle through changing biomes and take on boss stages
- Upgrade with training, skills, gear and rebirth for a bigger multiplier every run
- Daily quests, login rewards, a lucky spin and a season pass
- Join a clan and climb the global leaderboard
- Optional rewarded ads only. No pop-ups, and never during combat

Ready to bark orders?

**Keywords / tags:** idle, merge, shooter, dogs, zombies, robots, clicker, RPG, defense
**Category:** Games > Role Playing (or Casual / Simulation)
