Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version. Woof Wars has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. We do not ask for your email, location, contacts or photos. Your game progress stays on your device. If you pick a leaderboard name, that name and your best stage are shared with the leaderboard. Ads are optional and, in the mobile app, are provided by Google AdMob.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stage, weapons, upgrades, settings) is saved in your device's local storage so the game can remember it. We never receive it.

What the leaderboard collects

If you open the Leaderboard and choose a name, the game sends these to our leaderboard service (Supabase):

  • a random player ID made by the game (not linked to you or your device)
  • the display name you chose from the list the game offered
  • your highest stage and number of rebirths

This information is visible to other players on the leaderboard (name, stage and rebirths). The player ID is not shown. We use it only to show and rank scores and to limit abuse.

Ads (mobile app)

The mobile app shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google and its ad partners may collect device and usage information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interactions, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, you will be asked for consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The web version at this site does not load an ad network.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data.
  • We do not require an account.

Children

Woof Wars is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used a leaderboard name, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Do not open the Leaderboard, and nothing is sent to it.
  • To remove your leaderboard entry, contact us (below) and include your chosen display name. We will delete it.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes your on-device progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. Please do not include private information in a public issue; ask for a private way to continue and we will arrange one.
