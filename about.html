Woof Wars

A dog-powered idle merge shooter.

A pack of brave dogs holds the line against zombies and robots. Buy weapons, merge matching pairs into better ones, and watch your pack fight on its own, even while you are away. Climb through biomes, take on bosses, and see how far your pack can go.

Play now

What you will find

  • A 13-step weapon ladder, from a thrown rock to a grenade launcher
  • A growing pack of dog guardians, each with its own look and role
  • Boss stages, raids, daily quests, a season pass and a lucky spin
  • A global leaderboard, ranked by highest stage
  • Offline progress, so your pack keeps earning when you leave

Ads

Ads are always optional. You only ever see one if you tap a "watch an ad" button, and you get a reward for it. There are no pop-ups and no ads during combat.
